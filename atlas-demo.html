Sysible Atlas · concept

A companion terminal that reads your session.

Not another chat box glued to your prompt. Sysible Atlas is a second terminal that watches the first — it catches a failed command the moment it happens (no copy‑paste), and answers questions you ask inline. Runs entirely on a local model. Nothing leaves the machine.
